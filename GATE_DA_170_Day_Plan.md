# GATE DA 176-Day Execution Plan

This plan is designed for the 176 calendar days from 16 August 2026 through 7 February 2027, counting 16 August as Day 1. It uses 24 weeks (168 days) followed by an eight-day final consolidation phase. It assumes roughly 4–6 focused hours on weekdays and 6–8 on a weekend day. If you have less time, preserve the order and reduce question volume; do not remove revision and tests.

## Non-negotiable daily structure

1. **Concept block — 90 minutes:** learn one small idea and write a half-page summary.
2. **Worked examples — 60 minutes:** solve without looking at the solution.
3. **Practice — 90 minutes:** timed questions from the current and previous topics.
4. **Recall — 20 minutes:** formulas, definitions, and algorithms from memory.
5. **Error log — 10 minutes:** record why every error happened.

Every seventh day is a lighter review day: revise the week, take a short mixed test, and repair errors. Do not turn it into a zero-work day unless health requires it.

## Phase 1: Build the base — Days 1–28

### Week 1: Python and arithmetic

Python syntax, variables, types, operators, conditionals, loops, functions, lists, tuples, sets, dictionaries, basic input/output. GA ratios, percentages, powers, and logarithms.

Deliverable: 25 small Python traces/programs and a one-page arithmetic sheet.

### Week 2: Python practice and counting

Recursion, slicing, mutability, exceptions, permutations, combinations, arithmetic and geometric series. Start verbal grammar: parts of speech, articles, tenses, agreement.

Deliverable: one timed Python set and one timed GA set.

### Week 3: Probability foundations

Sample space, events, axioms, complement, union, intersection, conditional probability, independent and mutually exclusive events, marginal and joint probability.

Deliverable: 50 probability problems, including at least 10 probability-table questions.

### Week 4: Random variables

PMF, PDF, CDF, expectation, variance, covariance, correlation, conditional expectation and variance. GA data interpretation with tables and graphs.

Deliverable: derive the mean and variance of Bernoulli and binomial; complete one mixed GA test.

## Phase 2: Probability, statistics, and linear algebra — Days 29–70

### Week 5: Distributions

Uniform, Bernoulli, binomial, Poisson, exponential, normal, standard normal, t, and chi-squared. Know recognition, support, mean, variance, and assumptions.

Deliverable: a distribution comparison table written from memory.

### Week 6: Statistical inference

CLT, confidence intervals, z-test, t-test, chi-squared test, p-values, errors, degrees of freedom, and interpretation.

Deliverable: 30 inference questions and an assumptions checklist.

### Week 7: Vector spaces and matrices

Vectors, span, subspaces, dependence, independence, bases, dimensions, matrix operations, transpose, inverse, orthogonal and idempotent matrices.

Deliverable: solve 20 short matrix-property questions without a calculator.

### Week 8: Linear systems and projections

Gaussian elimination, rank, nullity, solution types, determinant, projection, quadratic forms, and least squares.

Deliverable: five complete elimination traces and a projection derivation.

### Week 9: Eigen, LU, and SVD

Eigenvalues, eigenvectors, diagonalization, LU decomposition, singular values, SVD interpretation, and low-rank approximation.

Deliverable: one handwritten page explaining SVD as rotate–stretch–rotate.

### Week 10: First consolidation

Two days probability revision, two days linear algebra revision, one day GA, one mixed DA test, one error-repair day.

Deliverable: one 3-hour mixed test and a ranked list of the five weakest subtopics.

## Phase 3: Calculus, algorithms, and databases — Days 71–112

### Week 11: Calculus

Functions, limits, continuity, differentiability, derivative rules, chain rule, Taylor series, maxima and minima, one-variable optimization.

Deliverable: derivative table, Taylor table, and 30 extrema questions.

### Week 12: Data structures

Python implementation and hand tracing of stacks, queues, linked lists, trees, and hash tables. Learn time and space complexity.

Deliverable: implement each structure once and produce a complexity table.

### Week 13: Searching and sorting

Linear and binary search, selection, bubble, insertion, merge, and quicksort. Trace partitions and merges. Learn best, average, worst cases and stability.

Deliverable: trace each sorting algorithm on the same small list.

### Week 14: Graph algorithms

Graph representations, BFS, DFS, connectivity, basic graph properties, shortest path, Dijkstra, and condition recognition.

Deliverable: trace BFS and DFS on five graphs and Dijkstra on three weighted graphs.

### Week 15: Database foundations

ER model, entities, attributes, relationships, cardinality, relational model, relational algebra, tuple-calculus idea, SQL selection, projection, joins, grouping, aggregation, NULL.

Deliverable: write 30 SQL queries from plain-English prompts.

### Week 16: Database design and warehousing

Integrity constraints, functional dependencies, 1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF, file organization, indexing, data types, preprocessing normalization, discretization, sampling, compression, star/snowflake schemas, hierarchies, and measures.

Deliverable: normalize three schemas and draw one star schema.

## Phase 4: Machine learning — Days 113–140

### Week 17: Regression

Simple and multiple linear regression, least squares, normal equation, geometric projection, assumptions, residuals, multicollinearity, ridge regression.

Deliverable: derive OLS and ridge normal equations and solve five numerical examples.

### Week 18: Classification

Logistic regression, cross-entropy, kNN, naive Bayes, LDA, SVM, decision trees, Gini, entropy, and information gain.

Deliverable: one model-comparison table containing assumptions, loss, strengths, weaknesses, and scaling needs.

### Week 19: Generalization

Bias-variance trade-off, underfitting, overfitting, regularization, train-validation-test splitting, LOO and k-fold cross-validation, leakage.

Deliverable: draw the correct data flow for five cross-validation scenarios.

### Week 20: Neural networks

Neuron, activations, feed-forward networks, MLP, loss, gradient descent, chain rule, backpropagation, learning rate, and common failure modes.

Deliverable: perform one forward pass and one simple gradient update by hand.

### Week 21: Unsupervised learning

k-means, k-medoids, hierarchical clustering, agglomerative/divisive procedures, single/complete/average linkage, dendrogram interpretation.

Deliverable: perform two rounds of k-means by hand and one small hierarchical clustering trace.

### Week 22: PCA and ML consolidation

Centering, covariance matrix, eigenvectors, explained variance, projection, scaling decisions, and PCA limitations. Finish with a full ML revision.

Deliverable: compute PCA for a tiny two-dimensional dataset and explain why PCA is not supervised classification.

## Phase 5: AI, GA, and exam integration — Days 141–168

### Week 23: AI

Uninformed, informed, and adversarial search; BFS, DFS, uniform-cost, greedy best-first, A*, admissibility, consistency, minimax, alpha-beta pruning, propositional and predicate logic, implication, contraposition, quantifier negation, conditional independence, variable elimination, and sampling.

Deliverable: solve five search traces, five logic questions, and three inference-factor problems.

### Week 24: Complete revision

Use two days for probability/statistics and linear algebra, two days for calculus/algorithms/databases, two days for ML/AI, and one day for GA. Take two full mixed tests during the week.

Deliverable: complete formula notebook and final error list.

## Days 169–176: Final consolidation

Use the extra time for performance improvement, not new chapters:

- **Day 169:** full mixed mock A.
- **Day 170:** analyze mock A; repair the two most frequent error categories.
- **Day 171:** focused repair in probability/statistics and linear algebra.
- **Day 172:** full mixed mock B.
- **Day 173:** analyze mock B; repair algorithms, databases, and GA errors.
- **Day 174:** final formula recall, ML/AI comparisons, and a short GA set.
- **Day 175:** light mixed recall, documents, timing plan, food, and sleep preparation.
- **Day 176:** if this is the exam day, do only calm recall and logistics; if the exam is later, take one final light revision block and avoid a punishing test.

## Time allocation rule

For Days 1–112, use approximately:

- 70% DA concepts and questions;
- 20% GA;
- 10% revision.

For Days 113–140:

- 75% ML and AI foundations;
- 15% mixed revision;
- 10% GA.

For Days 141–168:

- 45% mixed DA;
- 25% weak-topic repair;
- 20% GA;
- 10% formula and error-log recall.

## If a day is lost

Do not shift the entire plan and create panic. Use this recovery rule:

1. preserve the weekly review day;
2. remove enrichment reading first;
3. combine only closely related small subtopics;
4. never remove a scheduled test without replacing it;
5. use Days 169–176 for genuine final consolidation and only for disruptions that would otherwise damage the final revision.

The objective is stable repeated performance, not a heroic one-week burst.
